# Application Naming Concepts: Learning from the Past

A curated list of terms, synonyms, and concepts representing systems or entities that use past history, data, and failures to guide future actions and prevent recurring mistakes.

## 🏛️ Latin & Classical Roots (Authoritative & Professional)
* **Monitus** – Meaning "warning," "advice," or "remembrance."
* **Praemon** – Short for *Praemonitus* (forewarned).
* **Nestor** – Named after the wise mythological Greek king known for advising the younger generation using his decades of experience.
* **Memento** – Meaning "remember." It implies remembering past events to guide current actions.
* **Providentia** – Meaning "foresight" or "timely care."

## 🇯🇵 Japanese Concepts (Nuanced & Tech-Friendly)
* **Hansei (反省)** – The cultural practice of **introspection and acknowledging past mistakes** to prevent them from happening again. It is highly applicable to agile software development.
* **Kaizen (改善)** – Meaning "continuous improvement," which relies heavily on learning from past data.
* **Onko (温故)** – Derived from the proverb *"Onko Chishin"* (温故知新), which means **learning new things by reviewing the past**.

## 🇩🇪 German Compound Words (Deep & Precise)
* **Vorsicht** – Meaning "caution," "prudence," or "foresight."
* **Mahnmal** – Historically a monument that serves as a **warning from the past** to prevent future atrocities or mistakes.
* **Rückblick** – Meaning "retrospective" or "look-back."

## 🧭 English Synonyms & Modern Tech Names
* **Sentinel** – A guard or watchman that keeps watch to prevent danger.
* **Beacon** – A guiding light that helps ships avoid dangerous rocks based on historical shipping hazards.
* **Retrospect / Retro** – Directly tied to looking back at past sprints or project phases to capture lessons learned.
* **Forebear / Aegis** – Implying ancestral wisdom or a protective shield built from experience.
